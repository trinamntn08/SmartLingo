# Vocabulary photo coverage

Reviewed 2026-11-15. 397/705 exact current ID/target pairs have photographs (116 literal, 281 contextual); 308 remain explicit gaps. 120 distinct local JPEG assets total 7,802,322 bytes. Coverage is deliberately sense-specific; no generic image is assigned to unrelated words to inflate the count.

Context photos describe the visible scene and are explicitly prefixed “Context photograph:”. They offer a possible task setting, not proof of an abstract meaning, a person's identity/role, a diagnosis or a depicted conversation. Text meanings/examples remain authoritative. Numbers, connectors and some time-relative expressions particularly need authored visual treatment; many other concrete objects still need a suitable curated photograph. Calendar contexts show a physical calendar for date/planning language, not the specific target date; clock contexts similarly do not claim that the dial displays every target time. Gaps retain the existing drawing/text fallback. All photos were decoded in Chromium and contact sheets visually reviewed; this is a bounded visual/sense review, not owner publication approval.

The lookup requires the exact stable ID and target string; editing a target removes its photo until its mapping is deliberately reviewed. Multiple current targets on one canonical ID are separate entries. Personal/unknown/inherited-property IDs do not acquire photos.
