# Extend the French courses and topics

Started 2026-11-24. Owner is the primary agent for assembly, navigation, verification and Git handoff.

## Learner outcome

French Today and Learn offer three guided courses. A1 completes the workshop unit and begins neighbourhood directions. A2 starts with changing an appointment. B1 starts with handling a public transport disruption. Each new lesson also appears in its situation's topic bank, with a bilingual model for an optional spoken or written response. Learners can choose any level and return to their own saved lesson and step.

| Course | First batch | Communicative progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | D006–D009 | Ask for a room, complete a workshop check-in, find a nearby service and locate it using a landmark. Recycle names, spelling, time and clarification. |
| A2 | D057–D060 | Ask to move an appointment, explain the conflict, propose a workable time and check a confirmation. Recycle the A1 time and repair toolkit. |
| B1 | D113–D116 | Interpret a disruption, check an alternative, compare routes using a deadline, and relay the cause, revised arrival and contingency. Recycle directions, time and A2 schedule changes. |

These mappings continue the [A1–B1 roadmap](0043-french-daily-course.md). The Council of Europe distinguishes A1 concrete, cooperative exchanges, A2 routine practical exchanges and B1 connected language with brief reasons about familiar situations and travel. This informed the different response demands for each level. [CEFR global scale, accessed 2026-11-24](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale)

- **A1.** The references support introducing names and spelling, then locating places with short direction phrases and landmarks. ## Ownership and assembly

- Three curriculum-author agents own their respective A1, A2 and B1 topic modules and follow the French researcher and author skills. Each drafts four original lessons with canonical vocabulary links, bilingual study, guided practice and a new-situation response.
- The content package builds each topic once. The guided reader uses the same draft snapshot and preserves separate course progress IDs. The five existing A1 lesson revisions, published lesson overrides and Japanese course remain compatible. Early topic banks are intentionally compact.
- A2 and B1 have distinct course IDs. Their links resume independently through existing course events. Authored vocabulary, dialogue and exercises use the existing schemas and offline package. Text supplies all information needed to study. Playback uses the existing device-dependent support.
- The editor accepts the B1 label on French topic drafts. Scripted speaking covers all three levels. Optional AI conversation uses its existing foundation–A2 service contract and offers lessons within those levels.
