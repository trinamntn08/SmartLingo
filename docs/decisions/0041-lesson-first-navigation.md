# 0041 — Put the lesson on the main path

Accepted 2026-11-23 for the shared learner preview.

## Context

The topic browser put a long word bank before the lesson list. The owner asked for the authored lesson to be the main experience and for a learner to start within one or two actions from the homepage.

## Decision

After a language choice, Today places the recommended activity and its action above supplementary photography and browsing. French opens the recommended guided lesson or its saved section. Japanese offers kana when appropriate and keeps the guided lesson available.

Topic pages lead with short lessons. Topic vocabulary is a collapsed reference below the lesson list, with its current filter and expanded state tied to the browser visit. A fresh short lesson begins on the bilingual conversation. The sequence continues through key phrases, matching and sentence building. Learners with saved key-phrase study can proceed directly from the conversation to practice or review the terms again.

## Consequences

Existing stage routes and activity records keep their identities. The numbered navigation strip can open any stage without writing a learning event. Deliberately continuing from key phrases records the existing vocabulary review. Matching and the eligible correct sentence still determine practice and completion. Conversation photos follow the transcripts so the actual study material leads on small screens. The [execution plan](../plans/0052-lesson-first-learner.md) records validation.
