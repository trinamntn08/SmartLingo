# Per-bank curriculum review — draft for owner review

Navigation: [current course catalogue](../COURSE_CATALOGUE.md) · [authoring and publication guide](../CONTENT_GUIDE.md) · [review-pack overview](README.md).

Reviewed 2026-11-16. English-speaking adult newcomers to France; practical metropolitan French. This is original AI-assisted editorial work, **not owner approval, native-speaker review, certified CEFR coverage or an implemented learning path**. The app retains its current bundled lessons and ordering. All proposals and five correction candidates remain drafts.

The [progression manifest](topic-progression.json) assigns every existing lesson ID exactly once: **25 banks, 123 lessons, 7 foundation / 65 A1 / 51 A2**. It preserves current levels rather than silently reclassifying lessons. `foundationA1` combines the first two runtime labels for planning only. Empty routes explicitly describe a proposed bridge or revisit; they do not imply that lessons exist. First take the foundation/A1 pass in bank order, then revisit the A2 pass with the earlier support available. Recommended prerequisites and recycling edges only point to earlier banks; they are guidance, not access locks.

Each bank has two selected, task-essential subclusters using its existing canonical IDs. These selections are **not exhaustive bank inventories** and do not replace the [full vocabulary ledger](vocabulary-ledger.json). Each also has an original bilingual role-play or information-gap model, observable success criteria, a changed-context transfer task and bounded future B1/B2 outcomes. Models may use visible support chunks outside the selected target subset. They are editorial examples, not quotations or recorded authentic speech. No media was created or copied, and no course text was reproduced.

## What was actually reviewed

Inspected the runtime-resolved content for all 123 lessons: vocabulary targets, English meanings, French examples and English translations; article/gender and usage notes; full bilingual dialogues; grammar explanations; sentence prompts, tokens, accepted answers and explanations. Also inspected the builder/canonical alias behavior and current course sequence. The matching activity references were checked across all lessons: 121 use text-supported meaning matching, and the two original counter lessons use concrete bread/coffee picture prompts. This is a text/content review, not visual inspection of every image, audio listening, saved-editor preview, speech evaluation or learner trial. Sentence answer buildability and schema validation are mechanical checks, not proof of French quality. Existing accepted alternatives are preserved exactly in the correction candidates.

The earlier [741-occurrence placement audit](../CURRICULUM_TOPIC_AUDIT.md), [per-word report](../audits/all-topic-words.md) and [redundancy review](../CURRICULUM_REDUNDANCY_AUDIT.md) were reused as historical evidence rather than regenerated. Their keep/recycle/move/revise labels are editorial recommendations. They were not counts of confirmed French errors, and they predate the 24-expression Greetings addition. ## Five confirmed support defects and precise draft corrections

The current source still contains these defects. [refined-lessons.json](refined-lessons.json) contains **five complete schema candidates**, cloned from their current bundled lesson, with revision incremented by one and status draft. Only `grammar.explanation` changes besides revision/status. It includes exact before/after records. No vocabulary payload, dialogue, media reference, activity ID, token or accepted answer is changed. A later editor save must use the actual current head, not assume the bundled revision supersedes an owner's draft.

| Existing lesson ID | Confirmed defect | Proposed replacement explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fr.bakery.buy-lunch` | `Vous pouvez réchauffer… à Keep` has a corrupted question mark. | Vous pouvez + infinitive is an ordinary polite request: Vous pouvez réchauffer… ? Keep the action verb in its infinitive form. |
| `fr.groceries.find-products` | Three `à` characters replace question marks in the grammar examples. | Où est… ? asks for one place or item. Où sont… ? asks for a plural: Où sont les surgelés ? The accent distinguishes où (where) from ou (or). |
| `fr.restaurant.finish-the-meal` | Four `à` characters replace question punctuation in French/English question frames. | Je peux… ? means Can I…? Nous pouvons… ? means Can we…? Follow pouvoir with an infinitive such as payer or avoir. |
| `fr.housing.ask-about-a-rental` | `Les charges sont comprises à` has a corrupted question mark. | Quelles agrees with feminine plural charges. Comprises also agrees with charges. Learn Les charges sont comprises ? as a practical question, then ask what those charges cover. |
| `fr.work.organise-work` | The explanation says `la` replaces `tâche` in the dialogue, but no such pronoun occurs there. | Aller in the present + an infinitive describes a plan: je vais terminer. After tu peux, keep terminer in its dictionary form: tu peux terminer cette tâche. Tu is used between colleagues who already use familiar forms. |

The five defects were reconfirmed by exact lesson-ID queries and UTF-8 source reads; none has been applied to runtime content.

Every current lesson's exact assignment is in the JSON manifest. The inventory appendix below lists all lesson IDs reviewed. Findings labelled **proposal** are pedagogical refinements, not confirmed language defects. Where no defect is identified, this means no additional actionable defect was established in this bounded review, not blanket approval.

## Language and source confidence

The five defects above are directly reproducible from current bundled support. Level refinements, lexical additions and transfer tasks are **editorial proposals**. No exhaustive dictionary verification of all 722 canonical IDs, native-teacher judgement, actual audio quality or CEFR exam validation was performed. Open owner/language questions include acceptable beginner load in full job interviews, count/mass work-experience presentation, how early to teach ordinals/hundreds, and whether the selected A2-tagged everyday tasks should be rewritten as simpler A1 variants.

External comparison and actual retrieval records belong to the primary agent's [source ledger](sources.json) and [review-pack overview](README.md). This subtask did not independently retrieve new external pages or claim access to full proprietary courses. The primary reported inspecting Council of Europe practical-interaction descriptors and the Eaquals/CIEP inventory summaries; those support bounded communicative demands, not mandatory word-level assignments. Historical external references in prior repository reports remain historical. Source comparisons inform original choices; they grant no right to copy lessons, dialogues, lists or media.
