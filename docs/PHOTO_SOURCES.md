# Historical stock photo rights

On 2026-11-22 SmartLingo retired the 120 stock photographs from the learner and its offline export. Current app photography uses [SmartLingo-generated assets](MEDIA_SOURCES.md#generated-image-workflow). Old downloads and saved exports may still contain the previous 116 Wikimedia Commons photos and four Pexels photos. Keep those copies with their original credit page and licence records.

The last stock-photo checkpoint preserves each asset's source, creator, licence, changes and SHA-256. It also preserves the credit page and the original source audit.

- [Per-file source and licence manifest](https://github.com/trinamntn08/SmartLingo/blob/e35f03b39371368155571e84fe69b20136b3e9f5/apps/learner/public/vocabulary-photos/sources.json)
- [Original bundled credits page](https://github.com/trinamntn08/SmartLingo/blob/e35f03b39371368155571e84fe69b20136b3e9f5/apps/learner/public/vocabulary-photos/credits.html)
- [Original stock photo rights audit](https://github.com/trinamntn08/SmartLingo/blob/e35f03b39371368155571e84fe69b20136b3e9f5/docs/PHOTO_SOURCES.md)

For a new offline copy, download the updated app in Profile. The current app requests generated photos at new URLs, so files in a previous cache cannot appear in their place. The existing removal control can also remove older downloaded app assets while preserving saved words and progress.
