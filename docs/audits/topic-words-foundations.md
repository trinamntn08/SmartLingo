# Foundation and practical-life vocabulary audit

**Draft for owner review — 2026-11-15.** Research only. No runtime lesson, stable ID, exercise answer, accepted alternative, media, publication state or learner data was changed. Levels are editorial estimates, not certified CEFR coverage or native-speaker review.

Scope: 12 banks, 60 lessons and 360 vocabulary occurrences. The [JSON](topic-words-foundations.json) gives one disposition for every scoped `(lessonId, vocabularyId)` pair while retaining the current French target and English meaning as the baseline. Each assessment considered target, meaning, French example, English translation, article/gender, note/register, level and lesson outcome. Cross-topic decisions use the complete 120-lesson inventory, not ID prefixes.

`firstHome` means the proposed first teaching situation. `recycle` retains later contextual practice; `move` relocates first introduction but permits reuse. `revise` can mean an example, gender note or level should change even when the target is correct. Null correction fields mean retain those fields. Example-only recommendations are recorded below. The `prerequisite` tag includes unnecessarily late first teaching; it does not assert that every tagged item is too difficult.

The repeated five-lessons-by-six-items pattern creates artificial boundaries in dates, basic directions, digital access and study language. Keep short lessons while allowing task-driven bank sizes. Do not force 30 expressions or remove useful recycling merely to make totals look different.

## Essential conversation tools

**Outcome assessment.** Repair language is useful from day one, but it cannot replace an opening polite greeting exchange. Begin with **Everyday greetings and politeness** before transactions or repair. Existing `fr.bonjour`, `fr.silvousplait`, `fr.merci`, `fr.people.au-revoir`, `fr.essentials.excuse-me`, `fr.essentials.thank-you` and `fr.essentials.welcome-response` belong there first. **Bonsoir is absent from the entire inventory.** No fixed clock cutoff is asserted here.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Move first teaching of `fr.essentials.excuse-me`, `fr.essentials.thank-you` and `fr.essentials.welcome-response` to the opening bank; reuse them for help. Teach `fr.silvousplait` there first, then recycle. Teach oui, non and je ne peux pas as foundation chunks. Keep ne… plus at A1 after basic pas. Existing repair phrases, vous requests and the supported j’ai compris chunk are usable; no blanket rewrite is needed.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Bonsoir, informal salut with register support, bonne journée / bonne soirée, à bientôt and a short ça va ? response. Teach bonjour, bonsoir and s’il vous plaît in the first learning unit. Plain merci should precede merci beaucoup. Later add pourquoi ? with a short parce que model, and an immediate request frame je voudrais…. These are original planning candidates, not a required 30-item greeting inventory.

**Level/prerequisites.** Greetings → yes/no and polite requests → repair → questions → negative extensions. Provide alphabet support before épeler. Inversion in the current quelle-langue example is acceptable supported input; an additional spoken model can be offered during later exercise revision without deleting accepted alternatives.

## Numbers and quantities

**Outcome assessment.** All 30 targets fit number recognition, but almost every slot is a numeral: productive number building and actual quantities receive little practice. Keeping this bank at foundation/A1 is sensible.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Teach `fr.numbers.how-many` before the last hundred lesson. Keep zéro, but simplify its initial example (`fr.routine.zero`) to original **Le code commence par zéro.** / “The code starts with zero.” Current zéro place is understandable, not an alleged grammar error. Retain metropolitan seventy/ninety and the accurate quatre-vingts final-s note.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Practise number composition with vingt et un, vingt-deux, soixante et onze, quatre-vingt-un and quatre-vingt-dix-neuf; add mille for years/prices when needed. Teach un peu de earlier than its first Cooking occurrence. Select beaucoup de, assez de, trop de, plus de and moins de through usable quantity tasks. Recycle existing un kilo and un paquet from Groceries; consider un litre or une bouteille only for a concrete task. These patterns do not need separate new IDs for every possible number.

**Level/prerequisites.** Small counts → teens → tens/compounds → prices/quantities → phone digits and dates. The seize-heures example needs basic 24-hour-clock support; it does not make sixteen an advanced word.

## Dates and availability

**Outcome assessment.** Weekdays, months and nearby days fit, but twelve month-name slots delay the task language for dates and availability.

**Exact moves/revisions.** All six `fr.calendar.dates-and-slots` targets can first be A1: une date, un mois, une année, le premier, la semaine prochaine and disponible. Add masculine gender metadata/note to mardi, mercredi, jeudi, vendredi, dimanche and all twelve month names. Preserve bare targets and article-free specific-day/en-month examples; do not insert le into those sentences. Lundi and samedi already include gender.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Un an alongside une année; on est le combien ? / “What’s today’s date?”; le deux juin and numerical date reading; productive cette semaine and ce week-end; je suis libre… / je ne suis pas disponible…. First teach the existing `fr.social.free` availability phrase here and recycle in invitations.

**Level/prerequisites.** Numbers and nearby days → simple availability and dates → months as needed. Preserve août/aout variants. Contrast a specific weekday without le with weekly habits using le. Hier’s acknowledged past example can be a chunk; full past accounts stay later.

## Finding your way on foot

**Outcome assessment.** The mix of landmarks, relations and route verbs is coherent. Near/far and endpoint support should appear before they are assumed inside route examples.

**Exact moves/revisions.** All six `fr.directions.check-distance` entries can be supported A1 route language: près de, loin de, au bout de, jusqu’à, faire demi-tour and the walking-time question. Keep natural conversational à combien de minutes à pied ?. For `fr.administration.rue`, use original **La banque est dans cette rue.** / “The bank is on this street.” For `fr.directions.between`, use **La banque est entre la poste et la mairie.** / “The bank is between the post office and the town hall.” Current address/indoor examples are grammatical; these revisions sharpen the walking task. Teach mairie and bureau de poste as neighbourhood landmarks before procedure/postal transactions.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Simpler où est… ? before où se trouve… ?; je cherche…; c’est loin ?; c’est à cinq minutes; je suis perdu / perdue. Recycle à pied, marcher, pharmacy, bakery and station from existing IDs instead of adding duplicates.

**Level/prerequisites.** Polite attention, numbers and where-questions → landmarks/near-far → position → short instructions → clarification. Teach tournez, continuez and prenez as instructions before conjugation analysis. Spatial and temporal jusqu’à are related senses; retain their IDs.

## Cooking

**Outcome assessment.** Workspace, vegetables, seasoning, heating and storage form useful clusters with verbs. Isolated language examples do not establish a complete recipe or food-storage safety guidance.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Give `fr.cooking.a-little` an earlier Numbers and quantities first home. The six tools/instructions in `fr.cooking.control-the-cooking` can be A1 with chunk and time-number support. Also introduce servir, un couvercle, une boîte and mettre au réfrigérateur at A1; retain supported A2 work on laisser refroidir and fuller storage exchanges. Current garlic gender, partitives, wash-oneself contrast and poêle/stove distinction are sound.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Recycle Restaurant’s couteau, Groceries’ ingredients/oil and Home’s mettre/appliance language. Add un ingrédient, pendant dix minutes and a measurement chunk such as une cuillère à soupe de… when a complete recipe needs them. Chauffer, réchauffer or préchauffer should follow the actual recipe task, not a noun-count target.

**Level/prerequisites.** Quantities and mettre → tools/preparation → add/mix → cooking chunks → short sequenced instructions. Optional grammar may explain faire cuire after first practical use. Preserve cuillère/cuiller and conversational frigo support. No unverified temperatures or storage timings are proposed.

## Accommodation

**Outcome assessment.** Booking, arrival, supplies, room problems and departure are usable journeys. Supported routine problem-solving justifies A2 more clearly than a symmetrical level split.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Keep `fr.accommodation.book-room` and `fr.accommodation.complete-form` in Accommodation: these are **new contextual chunks** transferring familiar réserver/remplir verbs, not expressions already taught verbatim elsewhere. Link breakfast `fr.accommodation.breakfast` to `fr.routine.petit-dejeuner`; the same breakfast sense currently has two IDs and should not count twice as novel vocabulary. Preserve both IDs pending deliberate migration. The shared password is digital recycling and needs A1 availability. Retain related but distinct spatial/temporal jusqu’à senses.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** J’ai une réservation au nom de… is a core learner-side arrival chunk. Recycle une réservation, une clé, un étage, un ascenseur and basic room nouns. Add c’est complet if booking interaction requires understanding unavailable rooms. Dates/prices are prerequisites rather than more hotel-specific words to invent.

**Level/prerequisites.** Politeness, dates/numbers and rooms → booking/check-in; digital access and simple requests → supplies; missing/not-working frames → A2 problems. Existing room-label and identity-document notes appropriately avoid bed guarantees and universal check-in requirements.

## Education and learning

**Outcome assessment.** Enrolment and classroom actions fit adult learners. Basic attendance/homework vocabulary is overlabelled A2 while read/write and repair models are assumed.

**Exact moves/revisions.** For shared `fr.housing.open` in `fr.education.class-actions`, replace the key/door example with original **Ouvrez votre livre à la page dix.** / “Open your book at page ten.” Keep the general verb’s ID. All six entries in each of `fr.education.attendance` and `fr.education.homework` can be A1 with supported examples. Keep an A2 reflection cluster. **Keep des progrès:** its current example and note already teach faire des progrès, so no missing-chunk correction or noun-ID rewrite is warranted.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Recycle un cours, une professeure, un cahier, un stylo, un exercice and apprendre. Add écrire and use existing lire as a classroom instruction; reuse repair requests with classroom examples. Comment ça s’écrit ? may complete spelling interaction. Teach diplôme first with education/background, then recycle in employment. Une formation and un examen depend on the chosen learner task, not a quota.

**Level/prerequisites.** Repair and alphabet → listen/read/write/open instructions → enrolment/schedules → homework/practice → reflection. Je serai and a changé can be chunks or simpler A1 examples; they need not force absent or emploi du temps to A2. Preserve s’entraîner/s’entrainer.

## Finding a job

**Outcome assessment.** Work goals → applications → skills → interview → follow-up form a coherent A1-to-A2 path.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Keep une expérience: its existing note already distinguishes count experience from general de l’expérience, and the example demonstrates work experience correctly. Keep singular une disponibilité: the note already explains plural availability slots. These are valid dictionary forms, not confirmed defects. For recycled `fr.essentials.example`, use original **Vous pouvez donner un exemple de votre travail ?** / “Can you give an example of your work?” Teach diplôme first with education and se présenter with identity, then practise them here. Optional simpler chercher model: **Je cherche un emploi ici.** / “I’m looking for a job here.” Its current voudrais model is grammatical.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Recycle parler, habiter, envoyer, email/attachment and availability frames. Consider j’ai travaillé dans…, je sais…, je peux commencer…, je suis disponible à partir de… and combien d’heures par semaine ? within the relevant task. Introduce a small number of learner-relevant occupations before adding abstract synonyms; occupation choice needs owner relevance.

**Level/prerequisites.** Identity/present goals → availability/digital sending → A2 applications/skills → short past background/interview. Current contract/pay examples are language questions, not verified employment-law advice.

## Phone and digital communication

**Outcome assessment.** Calls, address dictation, messages, access and files are coherent. Basic Wi-Fi/password support should not be delayed until A2 when accommodation needs it earlier.

**Exact moves/revisions.** All six `fr.digital.connection` entries can be A1 supported access language; fuller troubleshooting may remain A2. Recycle personal email-address expressions from identity/contact details. Teach envoyer, connexion, réseau, mot de passe, se connecter and pièce jointe here before admin/work/job/hotel reuse. Keep `fr.digital.upload` target **mettre en ligne** and narrow its meaning to **“to put online / publish online”**; its example already fits. The phrase is legitimate. Generic transfer to a private destination is a different task and should not automatically imply publication. Keep the digital ouvrir sense ID while linking the general opening verb.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Allô ?, c’est de la part de qui ?, je voudrais parler à…, vous m’entendez ?, ça coupe and rappeler can strengthen actual phone exchanges. Add a complete fictional email-address dictation task. The files task assumes un fichier, absent as a target; consider un fichier and cliquer sur if needed for the complete journey. Avoid forcing every candidate into one lesson.

**Level/prerequisites.** Phone/numbers and alphabet → addresses/messages → A1 access → A2 files and connection problems. Link received/receive and envoyer forms. No real contact details or credentials are needed.

## Invitations and shared plans

**Outcome assessment.** Inviting, accepting, declining, organising and changing plans form a strong functional sequence. Teach familiar invitation words here at A1 and recycle in Leisure.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Keep apporter’s first home in Restaurant; replace its recycled fork request with original **Tu peux apporter une boisson ?** / “Can you bring a drink?” For `fr.work.ensemble`, use **On peut y aller ensemble.** / “We can go there together.” For `fr.education.prevenir`, use **Tu peux me prévenir si tu es en retard ?** / “Can you let me know if you’re late?” The si clause needs A2 support. For `fr.health.confirmer`, use **Je confirme pour le dîner de vendredi.** / “I’m confirming for Friday’s dinner.” First teach se retrouver, fixer une heure and à quelle heure as A1 chunks; time-questions belong with the clock. Ensemble itself is A1 support, while coordinating changes may remain A2.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Recycle proposer; add a short on va… ? or tu veux… ? suggestion. D’accord, bonne idée and à tout à l’heure can complete a response/closing. Treat volontiers as an alternative to avec plaisir rather than mandatory separate learning. First teach libre/disponible in calendar, then reuse here.

**Level/prerequisites.** Dates/time, leisure and refusal → A1 invite/accept/decline → A2 alternatives, notification and postponing. Keep tu/vous clear within dialogues and preserve accepted variants. Ça me va for plans differs from clothing fit; une place for a spot differs from a town square.

## Talking about recent experiences

**Outcome assessment.** All five blocks plausibly support A2 recent-event accounts. This is a justified A2-only bank; do not add lower-level slots for symmetry.

**Exact moves/revisions.** Keep shared d’abord and ensuite, but use original **D’abord, j’ai visité le musée.** / “First, I visited the museum.” and **Ensuite, j’ai retrouvé mes amis.** / “Then, I met up with my friends.” Their present meal/cleaning examples do not demonstrate the completed-event outcome. `fr.past.afterwards` is a valid adverb extension of routine prepositional après: link, retain both IDs. Keep `fr.past.memory` target/meaning; change its example translation to **“I have fond memories of that day.”** Current “good memory” may suggest memory ability. Personal-event and general work-experience senses are legitimate distinctions.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Provide optional support for j’ai…, je suis allé / allée…, être/avoir auxiliary choice and agreement inside the actual story. Recycle arriver, partir, rentrer and rencontrer with supported past forms. If the goal is exchanging stories, add et toi ? / qu’est-ce que tu as fait ? rather than many more isolated verbs.

**Level/prerequisites.** Present actions, recent dates and sequence → supported passé composé → useful reactions c’était/ça s’est bien passé → a short account. Do not imply mastery of every irregular or reflexive form. Preserve current feminine written alternatives.

## Preferences and simple comparisons

**Outcome assessment.** An entirely A2 bank hides basic preferences and connectors until late. Keep A2 comparisons and nuanced relationships while first teaching ordinary liking and reasons at A1.

**Exact moves/revisions.** A1 first teaching: préférer, adorer, détester, avoir envie de, choisir; à mon avis, je pense que, je trouve que, pour moi, c’est vrai, je ne suis pas d’accord; pratique, confortable, bruyant, calme, cher; parce que, donc, mais. The appendix lists exact IDs. Keep plaire at A2 with its subject/experiencer support; retain A2 productive plus/moins/aussi… que, meilleur/mieux, same-item agreement, pourtant, ça dépend and hésiter. Current agreement notes and comparative translations are generally usable.

**Missing essentials — candidates.** Recycle aimer before stronger liking verbs. Use je préfère X, je n’aime pas…, et toi / et vous ?, c’est bien / ce n’est pas très bien and pas cher before a broad opinion discussion. Recycle petit/grand and prices; bon/bien are useful explicit prerequisites for meilleur/mieux. Choose a short task over additional adjective synonyms.

**Level/prerequisites.** Aimer/basic choices → A1 reasons/qualities → preference → A2 comparison/explanation. An A2 task may recycle A1 adjectives without counting them as new A2 learning.
