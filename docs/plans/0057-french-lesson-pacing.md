# French lesson practice and pacing

Status: implemented in the local draft preview

## Outcome

Every bundled French topic lesson offers a practical response to a changed detail after the existing sentence exercise. Every guided lesson also offers its original contextual questions and a short role-play. Show a study-time estimate that includes the extra practice. Let learners move on at their own pace and preserve completion and published-lesson behaviour.

## Steps and ownership

- Curriculum authors own the two topic-practice data files. Match the actual lesson goal, vocabulary and difficulty and supply original bilingual models and self-checks.
- The primary agent owns the shared task contract, guided practice, learner UI, curriculum evidence, tests and preview. Guided questions use the authored choice blocks. Extra practice is for self-review and creates no learning or assessment events.
- An independent reviewer checks learner flow, technical regressions and a sample of the language.

## Validation

Check that every French draft has suitable practice and all five guided days have complete questions and an open response. Test the normal library and course paths, navigation, reveal and feedback, narrow screens and the downloaded app. Trial durations with adult beginners before treating the estimates as measured.

The final export passed the agreed checks across desktop and narrow browser sessions, including cold offline use.
