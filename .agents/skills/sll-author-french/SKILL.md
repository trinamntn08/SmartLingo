# Author French content

1. Read PROJECT_PLAN.md sections on learning, feedback, and content; read docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md.
2. Define a practical outcome, approximate level, situation, and learner role.
3. Reuse existing stable entries and senses instead of duplicating vocabulary.
4. Draft 6-10 useful items per lesson, natural examples, a short dialogue, and picture/sentence-building activities.
5. Supply directly visible English explanations and translations for study views. Keep recall reveals within review practice. Include articles/gender and register where relevant.
6. List valid answer alternatives and explain likely beginner mistakes. Distinguish accent feedback from meaning-changing errors.
7. Record factual/media sources and reuse permissions. Write original examples; do not reproduce a textbook or course.
8. Keep new items in draft. Flag uncertain French and estimated levels for owner review.
9. Validate schema/IDs/media references with actual content tooling when it exists; otherwise record the manual checks and tooling gap.
10. Deliver draft content and a concise review checklist. Never label unreviewed content as approved.
