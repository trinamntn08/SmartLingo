# French daily course A2 · Handle everyday changes

Draft planning map for adult English-speaking newcomers to France. The 56 lessons build from exchanging simple information at A1 to making routine arrangements, handling common problems, and giving short accounts and reasons. A2 describes the intended difficulty of these tasks. Coverage and level remain subject to language and learner review.

Each unit has five development lessons, one practice day that mixes earlier skills, and one transfer day in a fresh scenario. A transfer day checks whether the learner communicates the information the other person needs. Give feedback on missing or unclear details, intelligibility where relevant, and whether the language fits the situation. Offer a model response for self-review and a typed alternative to speaking.

Start from A1 introductions, time and dates, polite requests, directions, purchases and descriptions. Reuse the existing canonical senses where the meaning and register match. Provide owners with an exact vocabulary and source mapping when drafting each unit.

## Unit 1 · Change an appointment

Outcome. Rearrange a routine appointment and confirm the new details. Builds on A1 time, days, spelling and polite requests. A calendar, phone and community centre reception can be represented with generated imagery. Show the appointment details in accessible text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D057 | Ask to move an appointment | In a short call, identify the existing appointment and ask to move it. | `un rendez-vous`, `déplacer`, `je voudrais…` as a fixed request. Reuse the date, time, name and `vous`. |
| D058 | Explain why you cannot come | Give one simple reason and say when you are free. | `je ne peux pas venir parce que…`, `je suis disponible…`. Reuse time of day and weekday. |
| D059 | Offer another time | Ask for an earlier or later slot and propose one that fits your calendar. | `plus tôt`, `plus tard`, `un créneau`, `est-ce que… ?`. Reuse A1 time expressions and asking for clarification. |
| D060 | Check the confirmation | Find the new date, time and place in a short message and verify one detail with reception. | `confirmer`, `c’est bien… ?`, recognition of `reporté` and `annulé`. Reuse listening for numbers and spelling. |
| D061 | Say you will be late | Leave a short message with an apology and a new expected arrival time. | `je vais avoir du retard`, `j’arrive dans…`, `désolé(e)`. Recycle checking the time and giving one reason. |
| D062 | Practice • Match the message to the calendar | Tell which slot was cancelled or moved and identify what still needs confirming. | Reuse dates, time, simple negation and repair questions such as `Vous pouvez répéter ?`. |
| D063 | Transfer • Move a workshop intake | Use a fictional calendar conflict to request a new slot and repeat the final date, time and place. | Integrate polite openings, one constraint, a workable proposal and confirmation. |

## Unit 2 · Get there when transport changes

Outcome. Read or hear a simple service change, ask about an alternative and update the person waiting. Reuses directions and appointment time. Use a generated generic platform or bus stop. Render announcements and route choices as original text with an accessible timetable.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D064 | Find the change in a notice | Read a fictional service notice and identify the departure affected and what has changed. | `un retard`, `annulé`, `un changement de quai`. Reuse departures, numbers and clock times. |
| D065 | Ask about your connection | Ask where to change and check how long you have between services. | `une correspondance`, `il faut changer… ?`, `combien de temps… ?`. Reuse directions and clarification. |
| D066 | Compare two routes | Pick one option using a departure or arrival constraint and explain the choice in one sentence. | `plus rapide que`, `moins cher que`, `direct`, `parce que`. Reuse minutes, fares and arrival time. |
| D067 | Say what happened on the way | Tell an agent which service you took and which connection you missed. | First supported `passé composé` phrases with `avoir`, including `j’ai pris` and `j’ai raté`. Use a clear time anchor. |
| D068 | Update the person waiting | Send the reason for the delay and give a new arrival estimate. | Reuse `je vais…`, `dans…`, `parce que` and the late-message pattern from unit 1. |
| D069 | Practice • Read, listen and update | Extract the change from a short announcement, find the revised meeting time on a calendar and tell a classmate. | Mix route and clock information with appointment confirmation and a repetition request. |
| D070 | Transfer • Reach a meeting after a cancellation | Choose a feasible replacement from a new timetable, ask where it leaves and send the revised arrival time. | Combine notice reading, a simple comparison, a directions question and the short update. |

## Unit 3 · Fix an order or purchase

Outcome. Explain a simple mismatch and agree on a remedy with a shop or service. Reuses A1 shopping language, A2 comparisons and short past statements. Use a generated unbranded counter or product. Put fictional receipt and order lines in selectable text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D071 | Place an order for later | Give a quantity or size, place the order and check the collection time. | `une commande`, `commander`, `venir chercher`. Reuse quantities, `je voudrais` and time confirmation. |
| D072 | Check the receipt against the order | Locate the item, quantity and charge that differ between two short documents. | `un ticket de caisse`, `un article`, `une étiquette`, `vérifier`. Reuse numbers and the A1 price question. |
| D073 | Explain what is wrong | Tell staff what you ordered and describe what you received in two short statements. | `j’ai commandé…`, `j’ai reçu…`, `il manque…`. Recycle A2 past phrases and A1 descriptions. |
| D074 | Ask for an alternative | Ask whether a similar product is available and compare one feature or price. | `est-ce que vous avez… ?`, `une autre taille`, `plus… que`. Reuse price, quantity and polite clarification. |
| D075 | Request an exchange | Ask for an exchange and repeat a condition stated by staff in the fictional conversation. | `un échange`, `rapporter`, `pourriez-vous… ?` as a polite request. Reuse `c’est bien… ?`. |
| D076 | Practice • Clarify before travelling | Use an order message and an opening-hours notice to ask which item is ready and when to collect it. | Mix price, quantity and item details with unit 1 time confirmation and unit 2 notice reading. |
| D077 | Transfer • Collect an order with the wrong item | Explain the mismatch, ask for a suitable replacement and confirm the agreed next step. | Integrate short past statements, description, a simple comparison and a polite request. |

## Unit 4 · Arrange a repair at home

Outcome. Read basic housing information and report a routine repair with a clear location, duration and request. Reuses A1 rooms and objects and A2 appointments. Use generated interior context or an isolated leaking tap. Provide the fault description and repair slot in text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D078 | Read two housing listings | Identify room, floor and furnishing details and choose which listing fits a stated need. | `meublé`, `un étage`, `un ascenseur`. Reuse A1 rooms, `il y a`, and simple comparisons. |
| D079 | Ask what the rent includes | Ask about one unclear cost in a fictional listing and repeat the answer. | `un loyer`, `les charges`, `c’est compris ?`. Reuse exact numbers and confirmation. |
| D080 | Describe a fault and its duration | Identify a broken item, where it is, and how long the problem has been present. | `en panne`, `ne fonctionne plus`, `depuis` with the present. Reuse location and time expressions. |
| D081 | Send a repair request | Write a short message to a fictional property contact with the fault and a clear request for help. | `une fuite`, `une réparation`, `est-ce que vous pourriez… ?`. Reuse unit 3 politeness and D080 duration. |
| D082 | Agree on a visit | Offer a time window and confirm when the repair worker can come. | `entre… et…`, `un technicien`, `vous pouvez passer… ?`. Reuse unit 1 time and availability. |
| D083 | Practice • Decide what to tell the property contact | Use a fictional housing note and calendar to identify the contact, the issue and a suitable time to meet. | Mix listing vocabulary, repair description and appointment confirmation. |
| D084 | Transfer • Report a leak and arrange access | Describe a new fictional fault, give its location and duration, and confirm an offered visit. | Integrate the short repair message and time-window exchange. |

## Unit 5 · Communicate at a health appointment

Outcome. Give a brief description of a symptom, answer simple follow-up questions and verify a practical next step. Scenarios are invented language practice. Use a generated fictional reception desk or a learner-friendly illustration of a body area. Supply a text equivalent for each learning cue.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D085 | Say where it hurts | Describe the location of a fictional symptom and give one detail about when it occurs. | `j’ai mal à…`, `une douleur`, `quand…`. Reuse body words and routine time phrases. |
| D086 | Say when it started | Answer separately when a symptom began and how long it has continued. | `ça a commencé il y a…` for a start point, `depuis…` with the present for ongoing duration. Reuse D080 and `passé composé`. |
| D087 | Say what has changed | Compare how the character feels today with yesterday and respond to a short follow-up question. | `ça va mieux`, `ça s’aggrave` with English support, `surtout le matin`. Reuse time anchors and a single comparison. |
| D088 | Check a practical instruction | Ask a receptionist to repeat a location or timing detail and confirm it back. | `vous pouvez préciser… ?`, `avant`, `après`, `c’est bien… ?`. Reuse clarification and time. |
| D089 | Relay the next step | Use a fictional visit note to tell a friend where and when the follow-up takes place. | `la prochaine fois`, `un cabinet`, `je dois…`. Reuse directions, time and the confirmation pattern. |
| D090 | Practice • Duration, change and schedule | Distinguish two reported time frames and match each short message to its confirmed follow-up. | Reuse `depuis`, `il y a`, A2 comparison and unit 1 schedule language. |
| D091 | Transfer • Explain a fictional symptom and confirm the follow-up | Give the symptom location, onset and change in a short role-play. Ask once for clarification and repeat the appointment details. | Integrate one past fact, an ongoing detail and a practical confirmation. |

## Unit 6 · Follow up at work or on a course

Outcome. Ask about a learning or work arrangement, check instructions and give a short status update. Reuses appointments, practical clarification and the simple past. Use a generated classroom or workplace without branding. Show fictional instructions and schedules as text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D092 | Ask about an induction session | Read a short fictional invitation and ask one question about its time, location or supplies. | `une séance`, `apporter`, `être disponible`. Reuse unit 1 confirmation and A1 directions. |
| D093 | Mention relevant experience | Answer a short introductory question about one past course or job and one practical skill. | `j’ai suivi…`, `j’ai travaillé…`, `je sais + infinitive`. Reuse A1 introductions and unit 2 past forms. |
| D094 | Confirm what to do first | State two instructions in order and check a missing detail with a supervisor or teacher. | `d’abord`, `ensuite`, `il faut + infinitive`, `une consigne`. Reuse unit 5 clarification. |
| D095 | Explain a timetable conflict | Say which slot you cannot attend and suggest another that fits a fictional timetable. | `je dois…`, `je ne peux pas…`, `est-ce que je peux… ?`. Reuse the appointment change pattern. |
| D096 | Send a short progress update | Tell someone one task you finished, one still pending and what you will do next. | `j’ai envoyé…`, `je n’ai pas encore fini`, `je vais…`. Reuse past and near future from units 1–2. |
| D097 | Practice • Sort a busy day | From three short instructions and a timetable, state what you can do now and ask about the item still unclear. | Mix obligation, order, time constraints and polite repair questions. |
| D098 | Transfer • Confirm a revised training plan | After a fictional schedule change, confirm the updated slot and task, then send one sentence about what you already completed. | Integrate D094–D096 and confirmation from unit 1. |

## Unit 7 · Make plans and tell someone what happened

Outcome. Arrange an outing, adapt to a change and give a short account of a completed event. Builds from unit 1 scheduling and units 2–6 past phrases. Use generated images of a generic local venue or fictional participants. Provide the event and weather information in text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D099 | Invite someone to an outing | Invite a classmate, name a place and propose a meeting time. | `ça te dit de… ?`, `on se retrouve… ?`. Reuse dates, times and familiar register. |
| D100 | Decline and suggest another option | Decline one proposal politely, give a reason and offer an alternative. | `je ne peux pas parce que…`, `on pourrait…` as a proposal, `ça me va`. Reuse unit 1 time changes. |
| D101 | Change plans when the weather changes | Compare two fictional activities and agree on a workable indoor or outdoor option. | `il va pleuvoir`, `à l’intérieur`, `à l’extérieur`, `plus pratique`. Reuse comparison and offering options. |
| D102 | Put a past outing in order | Use an event timeline to recount two actions and one time or place detail. | `d’abord`, `puis`, `j’ai visité…`, `on est allé…` as a supported phrase. Reuse `passé composé` from earlier units. |
| D103 | Say how an outing went | Give one short description, one opinion and a reason after a fictional visit. | `c’était…`, `il y avait…` as background chunks, `j’ai aimé… parce que…`. Reuse A1 adjectives. |
| D104 | Practice • Tell plans from events | Sort a short chat about a possible outing and a later report, then tell a friend what actually happened. | Mix proposal phrases, `finalement`, near future, past phrases and one opinion. |
| D105 | Transfer • Leave a message about a changed outing | Given a fictional timeline, tell a friend what changed, what you did next and whether you enjoyed it. | Integrate one reason, two sequenced past details and one reaction. |

## Unit 8 · Organise a local activity

Outcome. Use two short sources to handle a familiar local arrangement and share a clear update. Reuses the whole A2 course and prepares the connected accounts and reasons of B1. Use a generated community hall or noticeboard. Build the fictional notices as accessible text inside the lesson.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D106 | Read a fictional registration notice | Find the activity, time, cost and one stated sign-up instruction. | `une inscription`, `s’inscrire`, `une place disponible`. Reuse prices, dates and `il faut`. |
| D107 | Choose a session that fits | Compare two sessions and choose one that fits a given schedule and travel time. | `plus tôt`, `plus près`, `pratique`, `parce que`. Reuse comparison, routine transport and time. |
| D108 | Ask for missing details | Write a short enquiry about a slot and how to reach the venue from a given stop. | `est-ce qu’il reste… ?`, `quel arrêt… ?`, `pourriez-vous… ?`. Reuse unit 2 travel and unit 3 courtesy. |
| D109 | Pass the information on | Give a classmate the confirmed time, meeting place and what to bring. | `on se retrouve…`, `il faut apporter…`, `à côté de…`. Reuse unit 6 instructions and unit 7 outing plans. |
| D110 | Say why the plan changed | Explain what prevented a character from attending, then state the new plan in a short update. | `j’ai raté…`, `il y a eu…`, `donc`, `je vais…`. Reuse unit 2 disruption and unit 7 sequencing. |
| D111 | Practice • Resolve a change and tell someone | Read a fictional cancellation and choose a valid replacement slot, then explain the change to someone waiting. | Mix source reading, reasons, clarification, time and a short message. |
| D112 | Transfer • Replan a first workshop visit | Use a fictional booking message and route notice to pick a workable session, confirm it with the organiser and send a clear update. | Integrate a constraint, a reason, the confirmed place and time, and the next action. |
