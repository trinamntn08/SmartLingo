# A1 French daily course — First steps in France

Draft roadmap for English-speaking adults new to French. Eight units provide 56 daily lessons, including the two existing pilot days. D001–D056 are planning references. The first unit starts with foundation tasks. At the end of the route, learners can practise giving simple personal information, making basic requests, and understanding short, clear replies in familiar situations. A1 is an estimate of the task demands for owner and qualified French review.

Each unit has five development lessons, one day that mixes earlier skills, and one transfer lesson in a new situation. The first unit uses D006 for simple directions while revisiting the opening exchanges. Story scenes and signs use fictional details. The suggested photo briefs describe scenes to generate with SmartLingo models. Instructions and activity information also appear in accessible text, including prices, names, times and directions.

## A1 Unit 1 — Your first conversations in France

**Outcome.** Check in for a first neighbourhood workshop session with a greeting, a name, a clarification request and an answer about time or place. Start with no prior French. Use register and examples from the existing pilot in D001 and D002.

**Generated photo brief.** A neighbourhood workshop reception desk, with a clear path through the doorway and a staff member welcoming an adult learner. Supply the room label and appointment time as interface text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D001 | Meet a neighbour | Greet a neighbour, check how they are and close politely. | Existing foundation pilot day. *Bonjour*, *comment allez-vous ?*, *bonne journée*. |
| D002 | Introduce yourself | Tell someone your name and ask theirs during a first meeting. | Existing A1 pilot day. *Je m’appelle*, *un prénom*, *un nom de famille*, *enchanté*. Reuse D001 greetings. |
| D003 | Spell your name | Say the letters of a short fictional name and confirm them after a read-back. | *Vous pouvez épeler ?*, *ça s’écrit…*, *c’est bien ça*. Teach only the letters needed for the task and offer a reference alphabet. |
| D004 | Ask someone to repeat | Request a slower repetition and confirm one detail from the reply. | *Vous pouvez répéter ?*, *plus lentement*, *je n’ai pas compris*. Reuse names and spelling from D002–D003. |
| D005 | Confirm a time | Ask for a session time and read it back to the other speaker. | *À quelle heure ?*, *à dix heures*, *à midi*, *demain*. Use a small set of whole hours with digit support. |
| D006 | Ask where to go | Ask for a workshop room and identify it after a short reply. | *Où est la salle ?*, *tout droit*, *à gauche*, *à droite*. Reuse *excusez-moi* and the repetition request. |
| D007 | Check in for a workshop | Greet the receptionist, give and spell a name, ask for one detail to be repeated and confirm the time and room. | Transfer D001–D006 with a different name, time and room. Use existing phrases and offer a model after the attempt. |

## A1 Unit 2 — Get around your neighbourhood

**Outcome.** Find a familiar local service and use a simple printed route to choose the correct transport. Prerequisites are D004–D006 clarification, time and directions.

**Generated photo brief.** A generic French neighbourhood with a pedestrian crossing, street corner and unbranded bus shelter. Put route lines, stop names and travel options into an original interface activity.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D008 | Find a local service | Ask where a library, a post office or a pharmacy is and select the relevant reply. | *Je cherche…*, *une bibliothèque*, *un bureau de poste*, *une pharmacie*. Reuse *où est… ?*. |
| D009 | Locate a place on a street | Tell a partner where a meeting point is using one recognisable landmark. | *À côté de*, *en face de*, *près de*, *un pont*, *une place*. Reuse D008 destinations. |
| D010 | Follow a short walking route | Follow two spoken directions on a simple original street diagram. | *Tournez*, *continuez*, *traversez*. Reuse D006 direction phrases and D009 landmarks. |
| D011 | Find the right bus | Ask whether a bus goes to a named stop and choose the line from two departures. | *Un arrêt*, *une ligne*, *ce bus va à… ?*, *je descends où ?*. Practise a few line numbers in context. |
| D012 | Ask for a ticket | Request one ticket and confirm the destination and whether it is one-way. | *Un billet*, *un aller simple*, *un aller-retour*, *pour…*, *je voudrais*. Reuse the destination and a short number. |
| D013 | Read a departure board | Match a destination and time to a departure and ask someone to confirm the stop. | Mix D005 times and D011–D012 route words. Gloss the board label *départ* on the display. |
| D014 | Get to the library | Find a departure, check where to get off, then identify the library from a two-step walking route. | Transfer D008–D013. Reuse D004 to ask for a repeated direction. |

## A1 Unit 3 — Buy food and pay

**Outcome.** Order food, get a quantity and price, and finish a short transaction. Prerequisites are D001 greetings and D012 *je voudrais*. Introduce quantities and prices in small sets.

**Generated photo brief.** An unbranded bakery counter and a small market stall with clearly identifiable bread and produce. Render sample prices and menus as independent text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D015 | Buy bread at a bakery | Ask for one or two items and recognise the seller’s closing question. | *Une baguette*, *un croissant*, *et avec ça ?*, singular and plural after quantities. Reuse greetings and polite endings. |
| D016 | Ask for fruit by weight | Request one weighed item and one counted item at a stall. | *Une pomme*, *une tomate*, *un kilo de*, *deux*, *trois*. Reuse D015 order forms. |
| D017 | Ask the price and pay | Ask what an item costs, recognise the total and choose how to pay. | *C’est combien ?*, *un euro*, *par carte*, *en espèces*. Reuse small numbers and add a limited set of prices. |
| D018 | Order a drink in a café | Ask for a drink and request the bill. | *Un café*, *un thé*, *de l’eau*, *l’addition*. Reuse *je voudrais*, *s’il vous plaît* and D017 payment language. |
| D019 | Choose a simple lunch | Read a short menu, ask what the dish of the day is and order one dish. | *Un menu*, *un plat*, *un dessert*, *le plat du jour*, *je prends*. Reuse D018 café politeness. |
| D020 | Check a food order | Listen to an order recap and gently correct one product or quantity. | Mix D004 clarification with D015–D019 food, numbers and prices. Practise *pardon, deux…* and *oui, c’est ça*. |
| D021 | Buy lunch for two | Select two items from a new menu, order the correct quantities and check the total. | Transfer the purchase and payment sequence with a new item that has a gloss. |

## A1 Unit 4 — Describe home and daily life

**Outcome.** Explain where you live, describe a few things at home and give a couple of details about a routine. Prerequisites are D002 names, D005 times and D009 location phrases.

**Generated photo brief.** Two generic apartment interiors with different furniture and a clearly visible entrance. Present a floor number, address and room labels as text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D022 | Say where you live | Give a fictional street address and identify the correct one after a read-back. | *J’habite…*, *une rue*, *un numéro*, *une adresse*. Reuse spelling and numbers from D003 and D011. |
| D023 | Describe rooms in a home | Say which rooms are present and ask whether there is a bedroom or a balcony. | *Un appartement*, *une cuisine*, *une chambre*, *un balcon*, *il y a*. Reuse D022 *j’habite*. |
| D024 | Find an item at home | Follow or give a short description of where a key has been left. | *Une clé*, *une table*, *sur*, *sous*, *à côté de*. Reuse D009 location phrases. |
| D025 | Say who lives with you | Introduce a member of a fictional household and say who lives where. | *Avec*, *seul / seule*, *un ami / une amie*, *une sœur*. Reuse D002 name phrases and D022 address language. |
| D026 | Describe your morning | Say when two usual morning actions happen and ask a partner about one. | *Je me lève*, *je prends le petit déjeuner*, *je pars*, *à sept heures*. Reuse D005 clock times. |
| D027 | Help a visitor find your door | Use a short voice message or its text to give an address, locate the entrance and confirm a meeting time. | Mix D022–D026 with D004 confirmation. Introduce *un étage* with a floor-number gloss if needed. |
| D028 | Welcome a guest | Respond to a guest’s questions about who is home, a room or object and when you usually leave. | Transfer D023–D027 via supported short replies with a new apartment and routine. |

## A1 Unit 5 — Make an appointment and ask for help

**Outcome.** Request a time for a routine appointment, share minimal fictional contact information and express a simple symptom. Prerequisites are D003–D005 spelling, clarification and time. Each health example trains language using fictional situations.

**Generated photo brief.** A generic reception counter with a calendar and blank appointment card. Supply the schedule and phone numbers in accessible text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D029 | Choose a day this week | Choose a suitable appointment day from two weekday options on a calendar. | *Lundi* through *vendredi*, *quel jour ?*, *ce mardi*. Reuse *aujourd’hui* with *demain* and D005 times. |
| D030 | Request an appointment | Ask for an appointment and accept or decline a time with a short reply. | *Un rendez-vous*, *prendre rendez-vous*, *je peux*, *je ne peux pas*. Reuse D029 weekdays and *à dix heures*. |
| D031 | Give a contact number | Say a fictional number in short groups and verify one corrected group. | *Un numéro de téléphone*, *mon numéro, c’est…*, *zéro* and selected two-digit groups. Offer digit-by-digit support for practice. |
| D032 | Say what hurts | Tell a receptionist or clinician one symptom and understand a simple confirmation question. | *J’ai mal à la tête*, *j’ai mal au ventre*, *j’ai de la fièvre*. Reuse *est-ce que… ?* in one familiar question. |
| D033 | Read the appointment reminder | Identify the date, time and address in a short fictional reminder. | *Le 12 juin*, *à dix heures*, *au 8, rue…*. Introduce the date pattern with supported month and number options. |
| D034 | Check the appointment details | Correct a name, number or time in a read-back and note the confirmed appointment. | Mix D003 spelling with D029–D033. Reuse D004 to request a slower reply. |
| D035 | Book a routine visit | Ask for a visit, mention a simple symptom, accept a time and select the matching appointment card. | Transfer D029–D034 through one guided exchange and a short reading check. |

## A1 Unit 6 — Start a class or a workday

**Outcome.** Say what you do, locate a class or workplace and clarify a short instruction. Prerequisites are D002–D006 introductions and clarification, plus D029–D030 availability.

**Generated photo brief.** An adult evening classroom and a small workplace entrance with everyday objects. Put room numbers and schedules in text beside the scenes.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D036 | Say what you do | Tell a new acquaintance whether you work or study and where. | *Je travaille…*, *j’étudie…*, *je suis étudiant / étudiante*, *un cours*. Reuse D002 name and greeting. |
| D037 | Check your schedule | Find a start time, day and room on a simple weekly schedule. | *Un horaire*, *un cours*, *commencer*, *finir*, *la salle*. Reuse dates and times from D005 and D029. |
| D038 | Ask for something you need | Request an everyday item and tell a partner whether you have it. | *Un stylo*, *un cahier*, *une feuille*, *j’ai besoin de*, *je n’ai pas de*. Reuse *vous pouvez m’aider ?* and politeness. |
| D039 | Understand a short instruction | Pick the action that matches one clear instruction and ask to hear it again. | *Écoutez*, *ouvrez*, *écrivez*, *vous pouvez répéter ?*. Recognise the instruction forms through guided examples. |
| D040 | Send a short absence message | Write a two- or three-line note saying who you are and which class or shift you will miss. | *Bonjour*, *je ne peux pas venir*, *aujourd’hui / demain*, *merci*. Reuse names, schedules and simple negative forms. |
| D041 | Find your first classroom | Read an updated room line, confirm the start time and ask where the room is. | Mix D006 directions, D037 schedule reading and D039 clarification. |
| D042 | Meet a new classmate | In a new setting, introduce yourself, find out where the class meets and ask for a missing item. | Transfer D036–D041 with an audio or text prompt and a short spoken or typed response. |

## A1 Unit 7 — Make simple plans with someone

**Outcome.** State a preference, suggest a familiar activity and agree when and where to meet. Prerequisites are D009 location, D026 routines and D029–D030 availability.

**Generated photo brief.** A public park, an indoor community space and a café entrance in the same visual style. Present activity and meeting options in text with an original weather card.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D043 | Talk about things you like | Say which two familiar activities you like and ask about one activity. | *J’aime*, *je n’aime pas*, *lire*, *marcher*, *écouter de la musique*. Reuse D026 simple activity statements. |
| D044 | Say what the weather is like | Describe the weather with a short phrase and choose an indoor or outdoor option. | *Il pleut*, *il fait beau*, *il fait froid*, *dehors*, *à l’intérieur*. Reuse familiar activities. |
| D045 | Invite someone to a simple activity | Suggest a café or a walk, then accept or decline an invitation. | *Tu veux… ?*, *on va… ?*, *oui, avec plaisir*, *désolé, je ne peux pas*. Use *tu* with a familiar peer and spot the polite *vous* alternative. |
| D046 | Fix a meeting place and time | Agree one meeting point and a time, then read both back. | *On se retrouve…*, *à quelle heure ?*, *samedi*, *dimanche*. Reuse D009 positions and D029 weekdays. |
| D047 | Write the plans in a message | Send a short message confirming what, where and when. | *Samedi, on va…*, *on se retrouve…*, *à bientôt*. Introduce near-future wording in one familiar model. |
| D048 | Find a plan that suits both people | Read a friend’s availability and weather card, then pick a suitable option and confirm it. | Mix D043–D047. Use two short replies and D004 clarification where helpful. |
| D049 | Arrange a weekend outing | Give a preference, suggest an available activity and send a one- or two-sentence confirmation in a new setting. | Transfer D043–D048 with a different activity and meeting point. |

## A1 Unit 8 — Handle newcomer errands

**Outcome.** Use French in a short sequence of services and transactions, with simple questions, confirmations and a short written message. Prerequisites are the prior seven units. Use mock forms and schedules with fictional data.

**Generated photo brief.** An unbranded service counter, pedestrian route and café. Provide editable mock service signs and printed details as original interface text.

| Day | Lesson | Learner task | Language focus and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D050 | Check when a service opens | Read a simple opening-hours card and ask whether the service is open at a chosen time. | *Ouvert*, *fermé*, *à quelle heure… ?*, *le lundi*. Reuse D029 days and D037 schedules. |
| D051 | Fill in a short registration form | Enter fictional identity and contact details into correctly labelled fields. | *Nom*, *prénom*, *adresse*, *téléphone*, *courriel*. Reuse D002, D022 and D031. |
| D052 | Get the right item at a counter | Check whether the counter has a specific item, then understand a clear yes or no answer. | *Vous avez… ?*, *une enveloppe*, *un timbre*, *je voudrais*. Reuse D015 quantities and D017 prices. |
| D053 | Ask a staff member for help | Say what you need, ask for the right desk and confirm the next step. | *J’ai besoin de…*, *vous pouvez m’aider ?*, *un guichet*. Reuse D006 location and D039 clarification. |
| D054 | Send a practical confirmation | Tell a friend where you will meet, suggest the time and name one planned stop on the way. | *Je vais…*, *avant*, *à…*, *on se retrouve…*. Reuse D046–D047. |
| D055 | Review a missed detail | Check a mock opening-hours sign and short voice message, then correct a wrong time or place in a reply. | Mix D050 opening hours, D034 correction and D054 confirmation. Support replay, transcript and a model after practice. |
| D056 | Run two errands in French | Find an open service, ask a staff member a question, then confirm a café meeting in a short message. | Transfer D050–D055 alongside D008–D014 navigation. Close with a self-check for asking, understanding and confirming. |

## Authoring handoff

Start with D003–D007 and verify prerequisites, language load and answer alternatives against the [French catalogue](../COURSE_CATALOGUE.md) and the existing [daily pilot](../../packages/content/src/open-course-pilot.ts). Reuse canonical senses for their existing meanings and give new meanings their own identities. In each development lesson, select a small group of useful forms, record which entries are recycled and provide learner-facing English. For days six and seven, supply a new scenario and a short evidence checklist built from earlier forms. Across the eight units, gather practical evidence from short spoken exchanges, simple reading and listening, and brief writing. A2 starts from the ability to make a simple appointment and confirm its day, time and place.
