## Research references

The Japan Foundation's official [Marugoto series overview](https://marugoto.jpf.go.jp/en/about/series/) and [teaching questions](https://marugoto.jpf.go.jp/en/teacher/qateaching/) were inspected for broad progression on 2026-11-19. They distinguish starter A1, elementary A2, a bridge A2/B1 and intermediate B1. The intermediate description covers several language activities and increasingly independent communication. These facts inform our own task design; we do not copy the books' sequence, lessons, dialogues, Can-do lists or downloadable assets. Download availability is not treated as redistribution permission. No JLPT equivalence is inferred.

## Draft route and review boundaries

Thirteen study days introduce foundations and then four days per estimated A1/A2/B1 level. Each has six to ten useful items, visible English meanings/examples, kana readings, study support, a dialogue, contextual choices and a manually reviewed production task. Later days add connected narrative, reasons/contrasts, interpreting messages and negotiating options. Earlier support is intentionally recycled; this short route is not a full course at each level.

Before publication, a qualified Japanese reviewer and owner must check naturalness, kana/kanji readings, translation, register, distractor ambiguity, prerequisites and actual difficulty. Readings preserve Japanese kana spelling, including particles; they are not pronunciation recordings. Foundation reading support does not replace a complete kana course. Choice success and manual completion record activity, not assessed speaking/listening/writing competence. No native review or independently verified CEFR coverage is claimed.
