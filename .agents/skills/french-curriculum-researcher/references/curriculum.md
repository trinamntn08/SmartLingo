# SmartLingo French topic framework — A1 + A2

The supplied package describes these as **original editorial seed clusters**, not textbook reproductions or official CEFR word lists. This integration does not independently establish their provenance or linguistic accuracy. Individual expressions need owner/teacher review, dictionary validation, and conversion into structured lessons before publishing. The seed catalog is a planning resource, NOT directly compliant with `content-schema.json` (which specifies richer research drafts, not runtime lessons). Split candidates into 6–10-item lessons and reconcile existing topic/sense IDs; see the skill's repository conversion guidance.

## Design

- A1: immediate communication, greetings, identity, personal daily life, purchases, location, simple health.
- A2: routine autonomy in France, transactions, explanations, past events, comparisons, appointments and problem-solving.
- B1 (later): narrative and connected discourse, opinions and reasons, everyday problems and experiences.
- B2 (later): nuanced arguments, complex practical/professional situations, viewpoints and trade-offs.

## Before content is published

For each topic split into 2–4 semantic/action subclusters, tag articles/gender/parts of speech, add English glosses and translated example sentences, check frequency and level, insert recycling links, design dialogue/activities and cite research. The optional `content-schema.json` validates research records only. Runtime lessons must follow the repository content guide and `lessonSchema`, remain drafts and pass owner preview/review before explicit publication. No seed is automatically approved or imported.
