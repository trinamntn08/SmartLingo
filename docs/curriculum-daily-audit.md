# Daily-life, education and job-search curriculum audit

**Historical authoring audit (2026-11-15).** Daily-life/education/job-search authoring rationale; the routine and paperwork replacements described in the later redundancy audit supersede the original number/calendar/internet task descriptions below. For the current bundled inventory and lesson order, see the [course catalogue](COURSE_CATALOGUE.md); for the latest full-payload review and separate unregistered/unpublished candidates, see the [curriculum review pack](curriculum/README.md).

Draft for owner review, 2026-11-15. Scope: the six banks in `daily-life.ts` and the two new banks in `study-work.ts`. All examples, dialogue and explanations are original editorial AI-assisted text. No lesson is approved or published by this audit; levels are estimates, not certified CEFR coverage or native-speaker review.

The reviewed learner journey is to find a relevant bank, read a short usable example with its English meaning, then practise a complete exchange. Each bank has five six-item lessons, exactly 30 distinct target forms and both A1 and A2 content. The existing 30 lesson IDs remain stable. A changed lexical sense gets a new ID; no old ID is reassigned to a different word.

## Existing-bank audit and changes

All 180 existing target slots received a fresh, shorter original example and faithful English translation. All 30 dialogues were reviewed and rewritten or completed. Existing sentence answers, pieces and accepted alternatives were preserved, including `soeur` and the punctuation variant for `Un café, ça te dit ?`. Existing correct noun articles/gender and sense IDs were retained. Optional grammar remains directly visible in English.

| Bank | Old weakness and precise remedy | Clusters and practical progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health and appointments | The booking lesson had five labels/time words and only `disponible` as a descriptive target; replaced `fr.health.apres-midi` (`un après-midi`) with new `fr.health.prendre-rendez-vous` (`prendre rendez-vous`). Booking now agrees a specific time. The consultation previously requested repetition immediately after answering; it now contains a new question, a repeat request, a simpler rephrasing and a response. Pharmacy now models disclosure and asking the professional to explain, without supplying treatment instructions. | Booking and availability; describing symptoms and their timing; clarification and rescheduling. A1 supplies the request and body-part patterns; A2 adds continuing time and a scheduling reason. |
| Work and study | Office nouns were useful, but the orientation dialogue stopped at directions. It now locates the responsible person and closes with an action. The study lesson had no stated relation to work; its stable `fr.work.study` ID now presents French training at work, starts an exercise and keeps its useful original material/learning targets. Document-help and clarification exchanges now resolve the request; planning distinguishes a meeting time from a reply deadline. | Orientation and workplace learning; document assistance; task planning and checking. The new education bank takes enrolment, attendance and homework beyond this introductory workplace class. |
| Services and administration | Contact details were six nouns and ended on an unanswered phone-number request. Replaced off-task device label `fr.administration.telephone` (`un téléphone`) with new `fr.administration.epeler` (`épeler`); the exchange now spells a name and offers to write the number. Postal vocabulary was six nouns; replaced `fr.administration.enveloppe` (`une enveloppe`) with new `fr.administration.poster` (`poster`) and asks staff which stamp is suitable. No eligibility, required-document list, postage rate or legal claim is taught. |
| People and introductions | The family lesson included friend/neighbour vocabulary; the clearer title “Introduce people close to you” makes that retained useful group coherent. Conversation support taught the broad adverb `encore` but no directly reusable repair formula: replaced `fr.people.encore` with new `fr.people.pas-compris` (`je n’ai pas compris`). English grammar now explains that formula. Origin, first meeting and settling-in exchanges now conclude with a response or next step. | Identity and origin; close people; conversation repair and neighbourhood contact. The two uses of `venir de` are distinguished: origin versus recent past. |
| Time and daily routine | Number examples wandered among unrelated counters, queues and journeys. They now support a small group size and short waiting times; the lesson outcome explicitly bounds this incomplete number set. No arbitrary noun or correct number was removed merely to increase verbs. | Small quantities and clock time; calendar planning; daily actions and sequence. This bank recycles with the larger new numbers/calendar banks; it is not a complete number or weekday inventory. |
| Leisure and weather | Activities were useful but some examples contained extra clauses, and weather/invitation exchanges stopped before agreement. Familiar `tu` is used consistently in the friend exchanges. |

The exact existing topic title for administration is retained in the module even where this audit uses the descriptive phrase “Services and administration”. No topic renaming is required for the rework.

They are explicit intentional recycling; the integrating owner controls alias definitions and canonical metadata. A shared ID must have identical metadata after integration. The first member in this list is a proposed anchor, not an instruction to rename any historical ID.

- `fr.education.ouvrir` = `fr.housing.open` (physical opening action, with different objects); `fr.education.prevenir` = `fr.social.let-know`; `fr.jobsearch.exemple` = `fr.essentials.example`. Digital `ouvrir` retains a separate contextual sense for opening a file or app.

The primary integrator retained contextual sense boundaries for the exact-target overlaps `fr.work.horaire` / `fr.transport.timetable` (an agreed work time / a departure or arrival time) and `fr.administration.guichet` / `fr.transport.ticket-counter` (a general service counter / a ticket counter). These are disclosed overlaps, excluded from the same-sense alias list above.

Distinct senses stay distinct: job-search `un poste` is a position, while administration `un bureau de poste` is a post office; education `un emploi du temps` is a schedule, while job-search `un emploi` is a job. `une expérience` in the skills lesson is a work-context use with the general word explained, not a claim of a separate homonym. `réussir`, `une disponibilité` and `savoir utiliser` are not alternate IDs for the different lexemes `terminer`, `disponible` or `connaître`.
