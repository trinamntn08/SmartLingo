# Task: Put lessons first

Status: complete
Owner: primary agent

## Outcome

A new learner can choose a language on the homepage and enter the recommended lesson on the next action. A returning learner can start or resume it from Today in one action. The main action and learning objective appear before supplementary media and browsing controls, including at phone widths.

Topic pages show their ordered short lessons first and keep the vocabulary bank collapsed until requested. A fresh short lesson opens on its bilingual conversation. Key phrases follow the dialogue before matching and sentence building. Existing links, saved attempts and progress remain usable.

## Steps and ownership

1. Adapt Today and its featured course card so the direct lesson action is visible immediately.
2. Make topic lesson cards the default and provide optional vocabulary on the same page.
3. Begin fresh short lessons with the conversation and guide the learner into words and practice.
4. Integrate labels, navigation, regression coverage and the stable preview. Review French and Japanese, desktop and phone, and cold offline access.
