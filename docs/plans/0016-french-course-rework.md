# French course coherence and expansion

Authorised 2026-11-15: use the French curriculum researcher to review and rework all topics, words and examples and expand a useful course.

## Acceptance criteria

- Audit all twelve existing topic banks and both starter lessons. Revise weak lexical clusters, overlong beginner examples, incomplete exchanges and unsupported difficulty assumptions.
- Add twelve task-led banks, targeting twenty-four banks and 120 short runtime lessons, with 30–50 distinct expressions per bank. Prefer useful chunks and actions alongside concrete nouns; do not fill quotas with unrelated words.
- Keep every lesson at 6–10 items with original translated examples, an outcome, a natural dialogue, grammar support and a solvable sentence activity. Foundation and A2-only banks are allowed when their demands justify them.
- Research against live Council of Europe guidance and two independent FLE sources. Distinguish broad inspiration from word-level evidence and editorial choices.
- Preserve lesson IDs and existing senses; use new IDs for replacements with a different sense. Document recycling and a suggested progression. All generated content remains draft.
- Append revision 3 only over explicitly fingerprinted untouched revision-1/2 seeds. Preserve owner-edited heads, publications and all historical rows. New lessons start at revision 1.
- Pass repository, application, relevant browser and export checks; obtain independent read-only review, update status/checkpoint and create a local checkpoint commit.
