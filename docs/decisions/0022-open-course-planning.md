# 0022: Plan an openly reusable course with direct website study

Date: 2026-11-17. Status: accepted owner direction for planning; architecture and curriculum implementation remain proposed.

The owner requested a plan for full Le Nouveau Taxi! 1–3 integration, with one book lesson per study day and all learning usable inside SmartLingo. After discussing reproduction rights, the owner clarified that obtaining publisher permission is not feasible and requested sources already reusable legally, with attribution where needed.

Preserve the full website learning and A1–B1 daily-course objective. Use an original SmartLingo course built from eligible open materials and original authoring; exclude proprietary Taxi reproduction and permission-negotiation work. Prefer verified CC BY resources, inspect individual asset exceptions and preserve credits/licence/change notices. Share-alike material is an optional supplement with its obligations tracked; noncommercial-only and unclear assets are excluded from the default course to preserve the planned commercial path.

The [implementation plan](../plans/0027-open-course-integration.md) recommends eligible legacy Français interactif and BCcampus companion material for a beginner pilot. It records edition/licence conflicts, media exceptions and the absence of a verified complete open A1–B1 replacement. Original intermediate curriculum and explicit B1 runtime support remain necessary planning work.

A course built from those sources has its own identity and progression. It must not claim to contain Taxi, reproduce the entire Taxi sequence, or certify a CEFR level. Direct website learning remains the target; an external-book checklist would not satisfy it.

This decision records scope and source-selection constraints. It does not approve actual source files, new lesson drafts, technical contracts, imports, publication, purchases or deployment. This task changes documentation only. Owner saved-revision review and explicit publication remain separate from agent research and tests.
