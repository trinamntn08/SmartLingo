# 0015 — Task-led curriculum rework and historical seed refresh

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: accepted for draft implementation; owner content approval remains pending.

The owner asks to review/rework the whole French topic/vocabulary catalogue and expand useful courses and examples. More entries alone do not establish a better course. Organise short lessons around observable tasks, balance concrete words with verbs and usable formulas, simplify beginner examples and finish dialogue exchanges. Keep directly visible meanings and English explanations. Level assignments are editorial estimates; foundation and A2-only banks are allowed. This supersedes the eight-word example minimum in ADR 0011: completeness and usefulness take priority over length.

Keep the sixty existing lesson IDs at revision 3 and start new lessons at revision 1. Retain IDs when a sense stays the same, and allocate new IDs for replacements with a different sense. Explicit aliases canonicalise reused senses; repeated bank appearances do not count as new senses. A companion suggested topic sequence documents prerequisites without locking free exploration or claiming a proficiency assessment. The runtime lesson schema and all learner stores remain unchanged.

Extend the existing opt-in seed fingerprint record to accept several historical baselines per lesson. Each baseline permits only its next consecutive revision unless it names an explicit `nextRevision`. The rework manifest records exact untouched bundled revisions 1 and 2; the revision-1 record explicitly targets revision 3. This supports installations that missed revision 2 without shipping old lesson bodies or fabricating intermediate history. The incoming revision must exceed the stored head. Append atomically only when the validated draft head's ID/revision/SHA256 matches a known baseline and the incoming destination is permitted. Preserve every historical row, owner-edited head, newer draft and publication. Corrupt heads roll back the batch; missing/untrusted baselines retain missing-ID-only seeding.

No automatic publication, owner database manipulation or learner notebook rewrite is authorised by content generation. Bundled drafts change after rebuilding; cached publications continue to take precedence, and existing downloaded copies require an explicit update. Existing local/API history and personal examples remain intact. Original media bindings may be retired for removed vocabulary; target guards prevent mislabelled images. New abstract expressions use honest meaning matching, with no fabricated picture coverage.

Research references support communicative scope, not an official word list. Source access and confidence, editorial decisions and remaining review limits are recorded in [curriculum research](../CURRICULUM_RESEARCH.md).
